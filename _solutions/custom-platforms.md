## The challenge

Off-the-shelf products rarely fit every operation. Combining devices, data storage and dashboards from different suppliers takes engineering.

## What we do

We build custom IoT platforms by integrating **sensors, controllers, edge servers, dashboards, databases and cloud services** into a coherent system. Our approach can combine existing technologies, custom hardware, software integration and purpose-built engineering.

Stay connected through real-time notifications and dashboards, and manage all your IoT installations from one centralized interface.

- One system for all your devices and data
- Data stored, organized and ready for analysis
