## The challenge

Fixed irrigation schedules waste water when conditions change and can fall short when plants need more. Water sources such as boreholes and tanks add another variable to manage.

## What we do

We design and deploy automated irrigation systems that combine **LoRaWAN soil sensors**, **valve controllers**, **borehole and water tank monitoring** and **on-site weather stations**.

Control logic runs on an **edge server on site**. It adjusts irrigation dynamically using real-time environmental data and the water that is actually available. An on-site weather station records **actual rainfall**, so irrigation schedules reflect the water the site has already received. The cloud adds data aggregation, trend analysis and continuous optimization.

## What you get

- Reduced water use, **up to 40% less water used**\* in one of our projects
- Irrigation scheduling informed by measured on-site rainfall
- Reliable irrigation that keeps running thanks to the on-site edge server
- Clear data on soil, weather and water resources, from anywhere
- A system that keeps improving as data builds up

<small>\*Results depend heavily on water supply, site and weather conditions, and are not contractual.</small>
