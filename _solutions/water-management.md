## The challenge

Water is a critical resource, yet tanks, boreholes and pipes are often checked by hand. Overflows, empty tanks and undetected leaks are only discovered after the damage is done.

## What we do

We build monitoring and control systems for water resources:

- **Tank level monitoring** with alerts before a tank runs low or overflows
- **Flow meters** to track consumption and spot abnormal use
- **Valve and pump control**, manual from a dashboard or automatic from rules
- **Borehole monitoring** to protect your water source

Local alerting and control run on the edge, so protection continues even if connectivity is lost. Dashboards and history are available remotely.

## What you get

- Live visibility of your water resources
- Early warning of leaks, overflows and shortages
- Less waste and better planning
