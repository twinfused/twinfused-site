## The challenge

Regional forecasts do not tell you what is happening on your site. Decisions about irrigation, energy or operations work best with local, real-time data.

## What we do

We install **on-site weather stations** and environmental sensors and connect them to your systems. Data feeds dashboards and automation logic, for example to adjust irrigation to actual conditions.

Data is collected locally on an edge server, with cloud integration for history and trend analysis.

## What you get

- Accurate local conditions, in real time
- Automation driven by actual weather, not guesses
- A record of conditions over time
