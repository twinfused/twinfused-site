## The challenge

A fridge or cold room that drifts out of range can ruin stock overnight. Manual checks and paper logs only show a problem after the fact.

## What we do

We deploy IoT systems for real-time condition monitoring using **LoRaWAN sensors** and **edge gateways**. Edge computing ensures continuous data acquisition and **local alerting**, and the system is resilient to loss of connectivity.

Cloud integration provides centralized dashboards, historical data analysis and remote access.

## What you get

- Continuous temperature monitoring, day and night
- Immediate alerts when conditions go out of range
- A history of recorded conditions you can review at any time
