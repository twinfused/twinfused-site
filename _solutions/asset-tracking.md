## The challenge

When vehicles and equipment are spread across sites and roads, it is hard to know where they are, how they are used and whether they are safe.

## What we do

We deploy **tracking devices** and the platform behind them for real-time tracking and condition monitoring. Applications include **GPS tracking** of vehicles and equipment and monitoring of their performance.

Data is centralized in dashboards, with history and remote access, so you can follow your assets from anywhere.

## What you get

- Live location and movement history
- Better use of vehicles and equipment
- Alerts when something leaves its expected area or behaves unusually
