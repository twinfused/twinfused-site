## The challenge

Cooling and equipment often run on fixed settings, whether a room is in use or not. Without measurement, it is hard to know where energy goes.

## What we do

We implement fully integrated control systems that combine sensors, automation logic and edge computing. They manage **legacy air conditioning (AC) and energy monitoring based on real conditions**, such as door status and occupancy.

**LoRaWAN devices** provide wireless, low-power monitoring, so no heavy rewiring is needed. The system operates autonomously at the edge, with cloud connectivity for remote supervision, configuration and performance monitoring.

We can also integrate other energy sources and systems, such as solar, so decisions are made on the full picture.

## What you get

- Air conditioning that responds to real use
- Clear energy data and performance tracking
- Control that keeps working without a permanent internet connection
