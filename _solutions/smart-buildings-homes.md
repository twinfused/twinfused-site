## The challenge

Buildings and homes are full of systems that work separately: lighting, cooling, security, energy. Making them work together is what turns a building into a smart one.

## What we do

We design connected systems that improve **comfort, efficiency, monitoring and control** for buildings and homes. We integrate different devices and brands so they can share data and respond to each other.

For homes, our dedicated **Smart Home** brand is built for everyday use. For commercial buildings, we bring the same approach to energy efficiency and security.

## What you get

- Comfort that adapts to how spaces are used
- Lower energy use and better visibility
- One place to monitor and control
