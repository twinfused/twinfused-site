## The challenge

Know the real water level at all times, and control pumps and valves from that data instead of by hand.

## The solution

We installed a full water level monitoring system using **submersible pressure sensors** to measure the level accurately. The system sends **alerts** when levels need attention, **closes valves automatically** and **controls the pumps**, so the water is managed by the data instead of by routine visits.

## The result

Reliable visibility of the water level, and automatic protection and control where it matters.
