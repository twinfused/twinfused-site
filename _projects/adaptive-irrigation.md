## The challenge

Irrigate efficiently and reliably while making the best use of the available water resources.

## The solution

We designed and deployed an automated irrigation system integrating **LoRaWAN soil sensors**, **valve controllers**, **borehole and water tank monitoring**, and **on-site weather stations**.

**Edge-based control logic** dynamically adjusts irrigation based on real-time environmental data and available water resources. An **on-site weather station** records the rainfall the site has actually received, so irrigation schedules are adjusted to measured rainfall rather than to a fixed timetable. Cloud integration enables data aggregation, trend analysis and continuous optimization.

## The result

Water usage was reduced, with savings of **up to 40%**\*, while keeping irrigation reliable and efficient thanks to the edge servers on site.

<small>\*Results depend heavily on water supply, site and weather conditions, and are not contractual.</small>
