## The challenge

Temperature-sensitive products and samples have to stay within range around the clock, and different units need different sensors: standard units, freezers and ultra-low freezers running at **-86°C**.

## The solution

We deployed a **LoRaWAN** monitoring network built around the **Milesight TS302** temperature sensor. Standard sensors cover the regular chillers and freezers, while dedicated **low-temperature sensors** handle the **-86°C freezers**, which need sensors rated for those temperatures.

**Door sensors** add the missing context: a temperature change is much easier to interpret when you know whether a door was left open. Everything is wireless over LoRaWAN.

## The result

One network for every cold unit, from standard to ultra-low, with temperature and door status in the same place.
