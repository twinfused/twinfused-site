## The challenge

Measure the real water level of a wetland, in a remote location with no mains power and no existing infrastructure.

## The solution

We deployed a **radar level sensor** at the water to measure the actual water level. The readings travel over **LoRaWAN** to an **off-grid gateway**, installed together with a **weather station** and powered by a **full solar system**.

An **edge server** on site makes the station fully independent, so data keeps being collected and handled locally.

## The result

A monitoring station that runs entirely on its own, giving a continuous record of the water level together with local weather conditions.
