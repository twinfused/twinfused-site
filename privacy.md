*Last updated: October 2026*

TwinFused Ltd ("TwinFused", "we") is based in Black River, Mauritius. This page explains how we handle personal data on **twinfused.mu**.

## What we collect

**Messages you send us.** When you email or call us, we receive your contact details and whatever you choose to tell us. We use them only to reply to you and to deliver the work you ask for. We do not sell them or share them for marketing.

**Website statistics.** We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used, for example which pages are visited, which countries visitors come from and which device they use. It sets cookies in your browser and sends anonymised usage data to Google. IP addresses are anonymised. We use this information only to improve the website.

**This site does not have accounts, forms or comments.** We do not collect anything else through it.

## Cookies

Google Analytics uses cookies to tell visits apart. You can refuse or delete them at any time in your browser settings, or install Google's opt-out add-on at [tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). The site works the same without them. Your light or dark mode choice is stored in your browser only and never sent to us.

## Third-party links

The site links to external services such as Facebook, LinkedIn and Google Maps. They have their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

## Your rights

Under the Mauritius Data Protection Act 2017 you may ask what personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Write to [connect@twinfused.mu](mailto:connect@twinfused.mu) and we will answer.

## Changes

If we change how we use data, we will update this page and its date.
